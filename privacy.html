Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what information may be handled when you visit the Age Calculator and how it is used. We aim to keep the service simple, transparent, and respectful of your privacy.

1. Information you enter

When you enter a date of birth into the calculator, the calculation is performed in your browser. We do not intentionally collect or store the date of birth you enter through the calculator itself.

2. Hosting and technical information

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit a hosted website, the hosting provider may process technical information such as browser type, referring page, IP address, and time of request to maintain and protect the service.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files that websites or their service providers may store in your browser. The calculator does not require an account to work. Third-party services, if enabled, may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies and settings.

4. Advertising

Advertising services may be used on this website now or in the future. If enabled, advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to deliver ads and measure performance. Please review the privacy policy of any ad provider you interact with.

5. Third-party links

This website may link to external sites. We do not control their content or privacy practices, so please review those policies separately before using those websites.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us or email othersideofme999@gmail.com.
