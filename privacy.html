Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what information may be handled when you visit the Age Calculator and how it is used. We aim to keep the calculator simple and transparent about privacy.

1. Information you enter

When you enter a date of birth into the calculator, the calculation is performed in your browser. We do not intentionally collect or store the date you enter through the calculator.

If you contact us, we receive the email address and any other information you choose to include in your message. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

2. Hosting and technical information

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit a hosted website, the hosting provider may process technical information (such as information sent by your browser) to deliver, maintain, and protect the service. See GitHub Pages information and GitHub’s privacy statement for details about its practices.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files that websites or their service providers may store in your browser. The calculator does not need an account to work. Third-party services, if present, may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies and settings.

4. Advertising

Advertising services may be used on this website now or in the future. If advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies, IP addresses, or similar technologies to deliver ads and measure their performance, subject to applicable settings and consent.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services or manage personalization through Google Ads Settings.

5. Third-party links

This website may link to external sites. We do not control their content or privacy practices, so please review their policies separately.

6. Children’s privacy

The website is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the calculator.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or its services change. Any revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
