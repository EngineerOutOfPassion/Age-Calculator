Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about calculating your age and using this tool.

Is this age calculator free to use?

Yes. You can use the age calculator for free, with no account required.

How do I calculate my exact age?

Choose your date of birth and select “Calculate my age.” The result shows your age in years, months, and days, along with your total months, total days, weekday of birth, and days until your next birthday.

Does the calculator account for leap years and different month lengths?

Yes. The calculation uses calendar dates, including different month lengths and leap years, to determine the elapsed time and your next birthday.

Can I calculate another person's age?

Yes. Enter any valid date of birth that is not in the future to calculate that person's age.

Is my date of birth stored or sent anywhere?

The calculator performs the calculation in your browser and does not send your date of birth to a server. For information about the website more generally, please see the privacy policy.

How is “days until next birthday” calculated?

It counts the calendar days from today to your next birthday. For a February 29 birthday in a non-leap year, this calculator observes the birthday on February 28.
