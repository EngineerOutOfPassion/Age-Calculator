About This Age Calculator

This free age calculator helps you find out how much time has passed since your date of birth. It is designed to make a common date calculation quick and easy to understand.

What it calculates

Enter your date of birth to see your age in years, months, and days. The result also includes:

  • Your total age in months and days
  • The weekday you were born
  • The number of days until your next birthday

How it works

The calculator performs the age calculation in your browser using the date you enter and your device’s current calendar date. It checks that a date is provided and does not allow a future date of birth.

Designed to be easy to use

The calculator is built to work across desktop and mobile screens, with a straightforward form and clearly presented results. It is intended for everyday information and should not be used as an official record or substitute for legal, medical, or other professional advice.
