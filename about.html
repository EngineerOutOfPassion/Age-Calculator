About the tool

About This Age Calculator

This free age calculator helps you quickly find out how much time has passed since your date of birth. It is designed to make an everyday date calculation simple, fast, and easy to understand.

What it calculates

Enter your birth date to see your age in years, months, and days. The result also includes:

  • Your total age in months and days
  • The weekday you were born on
  • The number of days until your next birthday

How it works

The calculator performs the age calculation directly in your browser using the date you enter and your device’s current calendar date. It checks that a valid date was provided and prevents future dates from being used.

Designed to be easy to use

The calculator is built to work across desktop and mobile devices with a clean layout and clear results. It is intended for everyday information and should not be used as an official record or substitute for legal, medical, or professional advice.
